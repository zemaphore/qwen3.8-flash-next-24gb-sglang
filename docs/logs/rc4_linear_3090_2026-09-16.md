## 2. TTFT and TG at fixed depth (`checks/tg_probe.*`)

Streamed 256-token generation on a synthetic repo prompt; the profile primes
the cache with a 16-token call first (so the measured request is a hit), the
control is cold. ITL from chunk arrival times (`stream_interval=1`).

| depth | arm | cached | TTFT | TG tok/s | ITL median / p95 | 256-token wall |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 35,550 | profile | 35,520 | **1.04 s** | 35.66 | 28.5 / 32.3 ms | 8.19 s |
| 35,550 | control | 0 | 14.66 s | 31.92 | 31.2 / 38.2 ms | 22.65 s |
| 131,100 | profile | 131,072 | **0.50 s** | 35.04 | 28.6 / 32.1 ms | 7.78 s |
| 131,100 | control | 0 | 56.34 s | 31.05 | 32.0 / 39.2 ms | 64.56 s |
| 248,700 | profile | 248,640 | **0.71 s** | 34.32 | 29.2 / 32.9 ms | 8.14 s |
| 248,700 | control | 0 | 113.68 s | 29.66 | 33.8 / 39.1 ms | 122.28 s |

- Warm TTFT is flat at 0.5–1.0 s from 35K to 249K; cold TTFT is 14–114 s.
- **TG is not degraded by the cache.** The profile measured 8–16 % *higher*
  TG than the control at every depth (35.7 vs 31.9, 35.0 vs 31.1, 34.3 vs
  29.7). That exceeds TG0's boot-to-boot variance (2.5–6 %) and is
  unexplained (candidate: the control's cold prefill leaves a different
  expert-residency/page-cache state than a hit; or the tree's page-64
  layout). It is reported, not claimed; replicate across boots before
  quoting it as a gain. The conservative statement is "TG unchanged".
- Cold prime cost on the profile (16.3 / 55.3 / 112.3 s) equals the control's
  cold TTFT, so the tree costs nothing on cold prefill.

## 3. Full-trace wall time, 12-turn growing session (`checks/trace12.*`)

Identical trace shape on both arms: turn 0 is a 6.3K cold prompt; each later
turn appends the model's 16-token output, a ~3.4K-token tool result and a user
line (final context 41.8K).

| | profile | control |
|---|---:|---:|
| turn 0 (cold) | 3.65 s | 3.79 s |
| turns 1–11 total | **20.5 s** | 119.9 s |
| median warm turn | 1.86 s | 12.38 s |
| turn 11 (41.8K) | 1.98 s | 17.60 s |
| **whole trace** | **24.1 s** | **123.7 s** (5.1×) |
| hits | 12/12 | 0/11 (by design) |

The gap widens with context: a warm turn costs ~0.6 ms per *uncached* token
regardless of prefix, while the control re-prefills the whole prompt each
turn. Extrapolated with the measured per-turn costs, the RC1-e 70-turn
session to 247K would be ~2.5 min on the profile versus ~2.4 h on the control
(sum of 70 cold prefills at ~1,500–2,400 tok/s).

## 4. Near-limit session and session-after-session (`checks/two_sessions_*`)

- Session A: **70/70 hits to 247,228 tokens**, median warm turn 2.15 s, min
  free VRAM 50 MiB at the last turn.
- Session B started immediately after A with **no flush**: **20/20 hits** to
  69.5K, free VRAM 212–356 MiB throughout. A's retained pages were displaced
  by ordinary logical eviction; because they were already physically backed,
  the RC1-d physical-pressure failure did not arise. This was the identified
  promotion risk for a single consumer and it passes.

## 5. What is and is not established

Established for the target workload (one linear conversation, one client):

- Full pool (262,144) retained; hits on every turn to 247K; warm TTFT
  0.5–1.0 s at any depth; TG ≥ control; cold PP unchanged; 5.1× lower
  full-trace wall at 12 turns / 42K and a far larger factor at depth; a new
  session after a near-limit one works; no errors in ~300 requests across
  the two RC4′ boots plus the RC1-e runs.
- Capacity ceiling: ~247–249K tokens of prompt (free VRAM 50–100 MiB at
  the end), versus the control's 257,456 smoke — about 10K tokens (the
  0.22 GiB of two extra recurrent slots).

Not established / caveats:

- The TG advantage (§2) needs replication across boots.
- Robustness outside the profile: forks, retries that branch, or a second
  client produce misses at 4 slots (hot tier 2) and, under sustained
  multi-conversation retention, the RC1-d crash modes. Stage RB (R1–R5 in
  the plan) addresses this; until then the profile is safe only for the
  single-consumer linear workload it was validated on.
- Numerical equivalence is bounded by RC1 §5.1 (a hit is indistinguishable
  from a recompute under the stack's own drift), not bit-exact.
- One boot per arm; the trace-level 5.1× is large enough that boot variance
  cannot change the verdict, but per-token figures carry TG0's 2.5–6 %.

## 6. Recommendation

Promote the profile as the **default for the single-consumer linear
workload**, keeping the frozen no-cache launcher as rollback, on these
conditions: (1) replicate the TG probe once more on a fresh boot; (2) record
in the launcher/README that the profile is validated for one linear session
only; (3) schedule RB (R1–R3 minimum) before any second client or forking
agent is pointed at the server. Promotion means changing the default launcher
to `--max-mamba-cache-size 4` without `--disable-radix-cache`; nothing else
in the accepted configuration changes.
