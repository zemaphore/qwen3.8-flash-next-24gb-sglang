# RC1: GPU-only hybrid prefix reuse (RTX 3090)

Date: 2026-09-16 UTC
Stage: RC1 of [RAM_PREFIX_CACHE_PLAN_3090.md](../RAM_PREFIX_CACHE_PLAN_3090.md)
Status: **gate passed for hit accounting** — real prefix-cache hits on the
accepted `int8ring_int4` + compressed-QSA + PLE stack at moderate context, with
repeated, appended, branched and A→B→A reuse all matching the page-aligned
shared prefix. A GPU hit is indistinguishable from a recompute under the
stack's own run-to-run drift (§5.1). **For the target workload (one linear
session) the 4-slot profile keeps the full 262,144 pool and hits on every
turn of a 70-turn session to 241,666 tokens, with warm turns of 1.8–2.5 s
(§5.2).** Multi-conversation limits: the
hot tier is `slots − 2` checkpoints, and retained lazy KV backing crashes the
server under churn at 8–16 slots regardless of chunk size (§3.1, §5.1).
No RAM transfer was added. The serving source is **unchanged** (diff SHA-256
`05f91d42...`); only a separate launcher enables the cache.

Base revision: `893047c` (RC0); serving tree `/root/sglang` at `73a255206f` plus
the accepted patch set. Raw evidence: `docs/logs/raw/rc1_3090_2026-09-16/`.
Probe/tooling: `tools/rc1_cache_probe.py`.

## 1. Runtime path (what actually runs)

With `--disable-radix-cache` removed and `page_size=64` (compressed QSA), the
built-in selection chain routes the hybrid-SSM model to `UnifiedRadixCache` with
`ComponentType.FULL + MAMBA`, not `MambaRadixCache`:

- `registry.py:121-122` (`is_hybrid_ssm` → `_create_unified_radix_cache`),
  confirmed at boot:
  `Tree cache initialized: source=default impl=UnifiedRadixCache hybrid_ssm=True`.
- `MambaComponent.__init__` (`unified_cache/components/mamba_component.py:61-70`)
  requires `enable_mamba_extra_buffer` for `page_size>1`; with `page_size=64` and
  `linear_attn_backend=triton`, `mamba_radix_cache_strategy=auto` resolves to
  `extra_buffer` (`arg_groups/overrides.py:1638-1646`). Effective argv records
  `mamba_radix_cache_strategy='extra_buffer'`, `uses_mamba_radix_cache=True`.
- Validation passed (`server_args.py:5823-5851`): `mamba_track_interval(256) %
  page_size(64) == 0`, CUDA + triton linear backend.

The extra-buffer ping-pong donation is what gives a cached node a recurrent
checkpoint; PLE state follows the same track slot via `_ple_track_targets`
(`qwen4_exp.py:349-385`) and `MambaPool.copy_from` siblings. This is why the
unmodified accepted patch already contains the working hooks once the tree is
enabled.

## 3. Memory and capacity tradeoff

First RC1 boot used `--max-mamba-cache-size 16`; the mamba pool grew to
1.79 GiB, the profiled KV capacity fell to 187,200 tokens, and during a ~6.5K
prefill the server hit a CUDA OOM allocating 54 MiB in the GDN extend kernel
(`chunk_fwd_o`) with 53 MiB free. The extra recurrent slots had consumed the
activation margin.

The pool was therefore capped at 131,072 tokens for this stage. This is an
experimental capacity tradeoff reported under the plan's "preserve 262,144 and
report any necessary tradeoff" rule; it is not a pre-authorized floor.

| Boot | mamba slots | mamba pool | KV capacity | `available_gpu_mem` | outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| RC1-a | 16 | 1.79 GiB | 187,200 | 2.75 GiB | OOM on ~6.5K prefill (original boot; log not retained) |
| RC1-a′ | 16 | 1.79 GiB | 187,200 | 2.75 GiB | **no OOM** on the same shapes (`server-rc1a.log`, 16:09) |
| RC1-b | 8 | 0.95 GiB | 131,072 | 3.59 GiB | stable (this report) |

**RC1-a re-run (16:06–16:11 UTC).** The original 16-slot boot log was
overwritten by the RC1-b restart, so the boot was repeated with
`SGLANG_3090_MAMBA_SIZE=16 SGLANG_3090_MAX_TOKENS=262144
SGLANG_3090_LAZY_TOKENS=262144` into `server-rc1a.log` (`rc1a_repro.txt`,
`launcher-rc1a.out`). The three boot figures reproduce exactly (log lines 43,
48, 58: `ssm_state size: 1.79GB`, `avail mem=2.84 GB` at pool end,
`max_total_num_tokens=187200`, `available_gpu_mem=2.75 GB`). **The OOM did
not reproduce**: a 6,069-token cold prefill, its repeat (6,016 cached), two
appended turns, and an 18,090-token cold prefill all completed
(`rc1a_probe_results.json`, `rc1a_probe_long.json`); zero
`OutOfMemory`/`Traceback`/`chunk_fwd_o` lines. Free VRAM fell to 46 MiB after
the sequence, i.e. the same margin as the original failure, so the 16-slot
profile is best described as *running with no activation headroom* rather than
as deterministically failing. The original OOM is retained as an unreproduced
observation; the RC1-b choice of 8 slots stands on the measured 46–53 MiB
margin, not on the OOM itself. The control was restored and verified afterward
(`launcher-control-restore.out`, `rc1a_repro.txt`).

Supported context for the RC1 profile is **131,072 tokens** (the model
`context_length` stays 262,144). Rejection of a >131,072-token request at
admission is assumed from `max_total_tokens`, not tested here. Restoring the
262,144 target needs either a smaller recurrent pool, a larger VRAM budget on
the accepted settings, or the RAM tier; that is a later-stage tradeoff.

### 3.1 Prefill-chunk arms at 16 slots (RC1-d, 17:00–17:26 UTC)

PP12 measured ~0.7 GiB more free VRAM at chunk 2048 than at 4608, so two arms
tested whether a smaller chunk restores capacity for the 16-slot profile
(`tools/rc1_chunk_arms.sh`, raw under `chunk_arms/c2048/` and `c3072/`; each
has `server.log`, `boot_lines.txt`, `long_cold.json`, `reuse_checks/`).

| Arm | profiled capacity | `available_gpu_mem` | hot tier (sweep) | cold PP 7.2K / 21.7K / 36.2K tok/s | churn outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 4608 (RC1-a′) | 187,200 | 2.75 GiB | — | — | not run |
| 2048 | 187,200 | 2.75 GiB | 14 | 1,038 / 1,741 / 1,910 | **server crash** at ~139K backed tokens |
| 3072 | 187,200 | 2.75 GiB | 14 | 1,121 / 2,173 / 2,294 | **server crash** at ~102K backed tokens |

Findings:

- **Chunk size does not change the profiled pool.** Capacity and
  `available_gpu_mem` are identical at 2048/3072/4608; the profiler sizes the
  pool from static memory and the slot count, not from the chunk. A smaller
  chunk only lowers the runtime activation peak.
- **Hot tier = slots − 2**, confirmed at 8 (6) and 16 (14). Consistent with
  `mem_cache/common.py:25-26`: a running request with prefix cache holds 3
  slots (2 ping-pong + 1 running), and the running slot is returned on
  completion.
- **Both arms crashed the server under multi-conversation churn**, in two
  different ways:
  - 2048 (`c2048/server.log:25325-25366`): `KV lazy backing: commit failed
    ... no headroom for 141312 tokens (free 637 MB, need 1549 MB)` →
    `Prefill out of memory` → scheduler exception → server exit. At that
    moment the tree held **136,320 evictable tokens and 12 evictable mamba
    slots**; the allocator counted 50,880 *logically* free tokens that had no
    physical backing and raised instead of evicting already-backed pages.
  - 3072 (`c3072/server.log:19683-19813`): `torch.OutOfMemoryError` on a
    36 MiB activation with 33 MiB free, immediately after a lazy commit to
    102,400 tokens with `torch reserved 12.90 GB`. This is the original RC1-a
    failure mode, reproduced.
  In both, the elastic expert controller had regrown `torch reserved` from
  ~11 GB to 12.65–12.90 GB while lazy backing grew, so the two consumers raced
  for the same headroom until one lost. A smaller chunk moved the crash
  point but did not remove it.
- The PP cost of the smaller chunk is as PP12 predicted: ~1,900–2,300 tok/s
  on 36K prompts versus the accepted ~1,540–1,940.

Conclusion: the chunk lever is worth ~0.5 GiB of activation margin but cannot
make a multi-conversation GPU hot tier safe at 187K, because retained lazy
backing is never reclaimed and the allocator does not evict on physical
pressure. These are RC3 blockers, not tunables.

## 5. Correctness

**Reuse is not less exact than recomputation.** Generated-token logprobs over 8
greedy tokens (`logprob_reuse.txt`), 6 independent prompts:

| trial | cached | max abs Δ cold-vs-cold | max abs Δ cold-vs-reuse | cold token ids match | reuse token ids match |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|:--:|:--:|
| 0 | 6848 | 0.96434 | 0.26255 | no | yes |
| 1 | 6848 | 0.10199 | 0.07145 | yes | yes |
| 2 | 6848 | 0.50527 | 0.17930 | no | yes |
| 3 | 6848 | 0.10168 | 0.07649 | yes | yes |
| 4 | 6848 | 0.12571 | 0.06892 | no | yes |
| 5 | 6848 | 0.27118 | 0.09656 | yes | yes |

The reuse delta (0.069–0.263) is **below** the unchanged server's own
cold-vs-cold delta (0.102–0.964) in every trial, and over these 8 tokens the
greedy ids are identical cold-vs-reuse 6/6.

**Why this bound does not transfer to RC2/RC3.** A GPU hit reads the *same
physical* int4 rows, INT8 ring rows and owner entries that the cold run wrote,
whereas a fresh cold run after a flush may land on different slots and a
different INT8/INT4 mix (RC0 §6). Reuse being closer than cold-vs-cold is
therefore expected, and it says nothing about a restore into new slots. RC2/RC3
need their own bound, starting from byte identity (RC0 §10).

**Baseline non-determinism is real and measured, and text diverges on reuse
too.** Identical cold requests separated by a cache flush differ in 4/6 trials
(`reuse_vs_cold.txt`); in the same file cold-vs-reuse text differs in 5/6
(including trial 5, where cold-vs-cold matched). A→AR→AR2 text mismatches in
4/5 (`repro_return.txt`); in `determinism.txt` §B trial 4 the hit differs from
its creator (A≠AR) but repeats itself (AR=AR2), and trial 2 differs on every
comparison. Greedy **text equality is therefore not a valid exactness gate on
this stack**, matching TG0's finding (long-logprob drift up to 2.37, not bitwise
reproducible). `probe.json`'s three text comparisons happened to match
(`all_reuse_same_output=True`); that is not evidence of exactness, and no
numerical-equivalence claim rests on text equality.

**Stability (original run, bounded claim).** 30 unique ~2.4K-token
conversations, each sent cold then immediately warm (`churn.txt`): 30/30 cold
misses and 30/30 warm hits, minimum free VRAM 616 MiB, no OOM/abort/traceback.
The ~75K retained tokens do **not** fill the 131,072-token pool, so this run
shows no KV eviction. See §5.1 for the run that does.

**Provenance.** `churn.txt`, `determinism.txt`, `logprob_reuse.txt`,
`reuse_vs_cold.txt`, `mismatch_detail.txt` and `repro_return.txt` came from
ad-hoc scripts that were not retained. They are superseded by §5.1, produced by
the committed `tools/rc1_reuse_checks.py`; cite §5.1, not those files.

### 5.1 Re-run with the committed tool (RC1-c, 16:24–16:45 UTC)

Same RC1-b profile, fresh boot (`server-rc1c.log`, `launcher-rc1c.out`), all
outputs under `reuse_checks/` (`run.txt` records args and nonce; each check has
a `.json` with full per-request data and a `.txt` summary). `max_new_tokens=16`,
`/flush_cache` before every cold run.

**Determinism** (`determinism.*`, 6 trials, identical prompt cold→warm→warm2):
c==w 5/6, w==w2 5/6. Warm hits 6,592/6,592 every time.

**Why greedy text diverges: a near-tie at the first token.** The test prompt
ends mid-conversation with no chat template, so the model's first token is
genuinely uncertain (top logprob −0.96 to −1.42, two candidates `\n\n` /
`\n` near-tied). In `logprob_reuse.json` identical cold runs pick different
first tokens in 4/6 trials and reuse matches *one* of the two colds. The
original text-mismatch counts (5/6, 4/5) measured this coin flip, not a cache
defect. Sampled-token comparisons on this prompt shape are therefore
uninformative; the teacher-forced measure below replaces them.

**Teacher-forced suffix logprobs** (`suffix_logprob.*`, 6 trials): logprobs of
the 63 uncached `conv_a` tokens after the page-aligned base prefix
(`logprob_start_len=6656`), cold1 vs cold2 (flush between) and cold1 vs reuse
(base cached, 6,656 hit), token ids identical by construction:

| trial | cold-vs-cold max / mean | cold-vs-reuse max / mean |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 1.486 / 0.128 | 2.095 / 0.153 |
| 1 | 1.725 / 0.158 | 1.515 / 0.127 |
| 2 | 1.205 / 0.124 | 1.718 / 0.118 |
| 3 | 2.182 / 0.134 | 0.948 / 0.107 |
| 4 | 1.205 / 0.127 | 1.820 / 0.142 |
| 5 | 0.742 / 0.104 | 1.463 / 0.121 |

Reuse-vs-cold is **inside** the cold-vs-cold envelope (means 0.107–0.153 vs
0.104–0.158; maxima 0.95–2.09 vs 0.74–2.18), not below it. The earlier claim
that reuse is "below" the server's own drift was an artifact of 8 sampled
tokens; the correct statement is that a GPU hit is *indistinguishable from* a
recompute under this stack's own run-to-run variation, which is itself large
(max 2.2 nats on identical cold inputs, consistent with TG0's 2.37). This is
the RC1 correctness result. It still does not bound a restore into different
slots (RC2/RC3).

**Hot-tier capacity is 6 conversations, set by mamba slots, not KV**
(`mamba_capacity.*`): A (6,597 tokens) cold, then k short unique
conversations (~730 tokens each), then A again. A is a full hit (6,592) for
k ≤ 5 and a **full miss (0 cached)** for k ≥ 6, at ~4.4K intervening tokens on
a 131K pool. With 8 slots, the tree retains 6 conversation checkpoints; losing
the recurrent checkpoint drops the whole prefix, there is no partial KV-only
hit. For RC3 this means the RAM tier must carry GDN/PLE checkpoints, not just
KV, and the practical GPU hot tier is a handful of conversations.

**Eviction then re-hit** (`evict_rehit.*`): A cold → 12 short fillers (8,772
tokens) → A: 0 cached (slot eviction, as above) → 25 × ~6.6K fillers (166,040
tokens, > pool) → A: 0 cached, 3.68 s → A again: 6,592 cached, 0.53 s, ids
identical to the preceding cold 16/16, max |Δlogprob| 0.378. Re-warming after
both kinds of eviction works and no request failed.

**Churn beyond the pool** (`churn.*`): 30 unique ~6.6K conversations, cold then
warm: 30/30 misses, 30/30 hits, ~199K tokens retained against a 131K pool, no
OOM/abort. But free VRAM fell monotonically 722 → 530 → 338 → 148 → **4 MiB**
as retained prefixes accumulated. `server-rc1c.log:5792-8692` shows why: the
lazy KV backing commits page by page up to the full pool (`owner backed` 528 →
960 MB, `driver free 0.33 → 0.00 GB`) because retained pages are never freed,
and the elastic expert controller bounces `torch reserved` between 12.51 and
10.97 GB to make room. The RC1-b profile therefore runs at ~0 free VRAM under
sustained multi-conversation traffic; the original 616 MiB minimum only held
because that run retained 75K tokens. This is the VRAM-reclamation problem the
plan flagged (`kv_vmm_backing.py` releases only at all-free) and it is now
measured, not hypothetical.

## 6. Findings and limitations

- **Linear-session profile is the deliverable.** 4 slots, chunk 4608, pool
  262,144, radix enabled (§5.2). The RAM tier (RC2/RC3) is not needed for
  one growing conversation; it is only needed for forks or interleaved
  sessions, which are out of the target workload.
- **The tree works on the custom pool for KV+full-attention pages.** QSA
  compressed keys ride the full-KV slot (`compressed_slot = full_slot // 4`) and
  were shared correctly across all hit patterns; the extra-buffer checkpoint
  carried GDN/PLE state.
- **The INT8 ring precision contract is untouched.** RC1 is GPU-only; the ring
  owner map is in-process and correct on the live slots. RC2 must still preserve
  INT8-valid rows across snapshot/restore (see RC0 §6).
- **No RAM tier.** Retained conversations live only in VRAM; the pool is bounded
  at 131,072 tokens and evicts under churn.
- **Capacity tradeoff.** 131,072 tokens vs the 262,144 target, due to the extra
  recurrent slots. Reported, not silent.
- **Exactness deferred.** RC1 shows a GPU hit is indistinguishable from a
  recompute under the stack's own run-to-run variation (teacher-forced 63-token
  suffix, §5.1); byte-level component round trips are RC2, and this envelope is
  not the RC2/RC3 acceptance bound.
- **Hot tier = 6 conversations.** Set by the 8 mamba slots; the seventh unique
  conversation evicts the oldest checkpoint and its whole prefix (§5.1). RC3's
  RAM tier must hold recurrent/PLE checkpoints, not just KV.
- **~0 free VRAM under retention.** Lazy KV backing grows to the full pool and
  is never released while pages are retained; free VRAM reached 4 MiB during
  churn (§5.1). RC3 needs granule-aware reclamation or a smaller committed
  pool before any multi-conversation benchmark.
- **Test prompt shape.** Prompts ending without a template produce a near-tied
  first token; use teacher-forced suffix logprobs (or a templated prompt) for
  any future comparison, never greedy text.
