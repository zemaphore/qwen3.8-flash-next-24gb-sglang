Presence is faster in every cell on both boots except full-boot prose 2K

(+11 % to +17 %) and persists at 32K and 128K (~+5 % to +12 %).

## Control conditions

Temperature (69–77 °C), SM clock (1,888–1,934 MHz) and free-VRAM minima are
equivalent across arms, while the mass arm draws slightly **less** power in both
brackets (246.8–267.2 W and 243.2–266.8 W vs 254.3–267.3 W and 259.5–270.5 W) —
the GPU waits rather than computes, consistent with more host-resident expert
reads. The two assets' top-184 sets overlap only ~131/184 per layer (mean), so
they are materially different placements.

## Relationship to the TG0-profile arm

The earlier run on the pre-promotion 256K radix-off profile
([`tg0_tg1_candidate_3090_2026-09-17.md`](tg0_tg1_candidate_3090_2026-09-17.md))
showed the same direction with a larger magnitude (+2.7 % to +67 %). On the
promoted radix profile the direction is unchanged and the magnitude is ~+5 % to
+17 %, with prose-2K at parity. The narrower spread and tighter free VRAM
(108 MiB vs 736 MiB at 128K) are consistent with the promoted profile's
different memory behaviour.

## Limitations

- Single bracket per arm (two presence, two mass boots); raw per-cell SD is up
  to ±1.3 tok/s, but the sign is consistent across every cell and boot.
- PP was not measured, so this is a TG-only tradeoff, as in the plan's TG1.
- With radix enabled, repeated same-prompt cells hit the prefix cache; TG
  excludes TTFT, so decode remains comparable, but the prefill/memory state
  differs from the earlier radix-off arm.
- Clocks were not pinned; power/thermal state was sampled only.

## Conclusion

Presence placement beats the pooled-mass asset on the promoted profile across
the TG0 workloads; the launcher default (presence) is already optimal here, so
**no change** is made. The pooled `expert_freq.pt` asset is workload-specific
(built from a different probe corpus) and should not replace presence without a
matched, bracketed win on the target workload.
